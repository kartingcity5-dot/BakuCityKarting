The Experience

More than a race — an adrenaline destination

From first-timers to seasoned pilots, every visit is engineered for speed, safety and a serious rush.

Pro Electric Karts

Instant torque, whisper-quiet, zero fumes. Adult, junior and race-spec machines reaching 65 km/h.

Safety First

FIA-grade barriers, full safety briefing, helmets and suits provided. Insurance-included sessions available.

Karting School

Certified instructors, kids' programmes from age 7, and racing lines coaching to shave off your lap times.

Live Championships

Weekly timed sessions, live leaderboards and monthly finals. Climb the ranks and claim the podium.

Rooftop Lounge

A panoramic restaurant & cocktail bar overlooking the track. Watch the action, then dine in style.

Events & Parties

Birthdays, corporate team-building and VIP celebrations — fully catered packages built around you.
